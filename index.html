Behind The Music

The Story of SYLASO

SYLASO is a Ghanaian music artist blending the energy of hip-hop with the infectious rhythms of Afrobeats. Driven by creativity, culture, and authentic storytelling, his sound brings African influences to a global audience.

Every record tells a story. Every performance creates a connection. From the studio to the stage, SYLASO continues to build a sound that reflects ambition, identity, and the power of music.

His achievements include winning Student Artist of the Year at the Cape Coast Technical University Eminence Awards and earning multiple nominations for Ditto Music Africa's Artist of the Week.
